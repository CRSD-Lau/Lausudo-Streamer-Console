# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers are expected to be respectful, constructive, and
privacy-conscious. Good participation includes:

- discussing ideas and trade-offs without personal attacks;
- giving actionable feedback and accepting correction gracefully;
- respecting differing experience levels and accessibility needs;
- protecting viewer, account, and stream-operator privacy;
- avoiding harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing,
  and publication of another person's private information.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other project spaces. It also applies when someone is publicly representing
the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when conduct is harmful, disruptive, or unsafe. Report conduct
concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile contact method.
Do not disclose another person's private information in a public issue.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, recurrence, and the safety
of affected people.
